## 1. THE WHY, WHAT & HOW

### Why This Exists

Local businesses lose 40–60 % of reachable revenue to three problems they can feel but can't fix with more hiring:

| Problem | Reality |
|---|---|
| **Missed leads** | 95 % of calls that hit voicemail never call back. After-hours inquiries go to whichever competitor answers first. |
| **Slow follow-up** | Industry-average first response is over 90 minutes. The business that replies in under 60 seconds wins the job. |
| **Zero follow-through** | 52 % of estimates, treatment plans, and proposals are never followed up — ever. |

These aren't character flaws. A 4-person HVAC shop can't answer the phone at 2 AM, follow up on 40 open quotes, and run service calls the next morning. The math doesn't work with humans alone.

### What We Sell

An **AI Workforce** — not a chatbot, not a single tool — a team of AI agents custom-built for the prospect's industry vertical.

Each agent handles one operational job:

- **Inbound capture** — answers every call, form, and DM in under 60 seconds, 24/7.
- **Appointment booking** — schedules, confirms, and reduces no-shows automatically.
- **Estimate / proposal follow-up** — re-engages every unsold quote on a cadence.
- **Reputation building** — requests Google reviews at peak satisfaction moments.
- **Reactivation** — brings back past clients who went dormant.
- **Payment chasing** — sends invoice reminders and collects outstanding balances.
- **Client updates** — proactive status messages so clients never have to chase you.

Every agent uses language, workflows, and KPIs native to the prospect's industry. A roofing agent talks about Xactimate supplements. A dental agent talks about hygiene recall and treatment acceptance. A law-firm system talks about demand letters and case qualification. Nothing is generic.

## 2. SALES DEMO WALKTHROUGH

This is a step-by-step guide to presenting the platform during a live screen-share.

### Pre-Demo Checklist

- [ ] Know the prospect's industry vertical.
- [ ] Research their company: website, Google reviews, team size, service area.
- [ ] Estimate their numbers (monthly leads, avg job value, team size).
- [ ] Have the demo platform loaded at `localhost:3000` or the deployed URL.
- [ ] Test screen share and audio.

### Demo Flow (25 minutes)

---

#### Step 1 — Open & Set the Frame (2 min)

> "Thanks for taking the time, [NAME]. I'm going to show you exactly how [COMPANY] can capture more revenue without hiring more staff. This isn't a generic chatbot — this is an AI team built specifically for [INDUSTRY]. If you like what you see, we can have it live within 24 hours on a free 7-day trial. Sound good?"

---

#### Step 2 — Select Their Industry (1 min)

Click into their vertical on the landing page.

> "We've built separate AI teams for each industry because roofing is completely different from dental which is completely different from HVAC. Nothing here is one-size-fits-all."

---

#### Step 3 — Your Numbers (Discovery Inputs tab) (3 min)

Enter THEIR real numbers into the sliders. Ask as you go:

- "Roughly how many leads or calls come in per month?"
- "What's your average job / case / treatment value?"
- "How many people are handling phones and follow-up?"
- "What would you estimate your close rate is right now?"
- "How fast does someone typically respond to a new inquiry?"

> "Now the platform is calculating YOUR specific revenue gap and ROI."

**What the prospect sees:** Right-side cards update in real-time — missed revenue, leads going cold, current close rate vs. projected close rate, recovered revenue, payback period.

---

#### Step 4 — Problems Tab (Pain Reveal) — 3 min

> "Before I show you the solution, look at what the data says about [COMPANY]."

Walk through each pain card. Read the headline stat, the description, and the dollar cost.

> "Does this sound familiar? Which of these hits hardest for [COMPANY]?"

Let them talk. This is where emotion enters the conversation.

---

#### Step 5 — Your Day Tab (Before vs. After) — THE MONEY MOMENT (5 min)

> "This is my favourite part. Watch a typical day at [COMPANY] — first how it looks right now, then how it looks with your AI team."

**Before timeline:** A lead calls → rings 4 times → voicemail → never returned → that $[AVG VALUE] job walks out the door.

**After timeline:** Same lead calls → answered in 47 seconds → appointment booked automatically → confirmation sent → follow-up scheduled → you show up and close.

> "This is happening [X] times per month based on the numbers you just gave me."

---

#### Step 6 — AI Team Tab (Command Centre) — 5 min

> "Here's the AI team we'd deploy for [COMPANY]."

Walk through each agent/system relevant to their vertical. Highlight:

- What each agent does in plain language.
- One example output (the AI-generated message or action).
- Why it matters for their specific business.

> "Every one of these agents is trained on [INDUSTRY] language and workflows. The [AGENT NAME] knows how to handle [INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC TASK] because we programmed it with the exact process that works in your world."

---

#### Step 7 — ROI Tab (3 min)

> "Let's talk money."

Walk through:
- **Money being lost** section — total monthly revenue gap.
- **Money being gained** section — projected recovered revenue.
- **Payback period** — how many days until the system pays for itself.

> "Your payback period is [X] days. Everything after that is pure profit."

---

#### Step 8 — Proof Tab (2 min)

Show 1–2 case studies from their vertical or a similar one.

> "Don't just take my word for it — here's what happened for [SIMILAR BUSINESS]."

---

#### Step 9 — Free Trial Close (3 min)

Navigate to the **Free Trial** tab.

> "Here's what I'm proposing. Let's get your AI team live within 24 hours. For 7 days, completely free. Zero cost, zero risk, zero commitment. If after 7 days you're not seeing results, you walk away. Based on these numbers, I think you'll see $[PROJECTED VALUE] in recovered revenue in the first month alone. What questions do you have before we get started?"

## 4. DISCOVERY CALL QUESTIONS

These are the questions you ask on the call — while entering their numbers into the platform — to uncover pain, quantify the gap, and set up the demo reveal. Ask 5–8 per call; prioritise the ones marked ★.

### Universal Questions (Every Vertical)

**Current state:**
- ★ "Walk me through what happens when a new lead contacts you right now — from first ring to booked appointment."
- ★ "What happens to leads that come in after hours or on weekends?"
- "How quickly does someone on your team respond to a new inquiry?"
- "How many leads or calls do you get in an average month?"
- "What's your average job / case / treatment value?"

**Pain:**
- ★ "What percentage of your estimates or proposals actually convert to paying work?"
- ★ "Who follows up on quotes that don't close — and how consistent is that?"
- "How many calls do you think you miss in a typical week?"
- "If you could fix one thing about your sales process overnight, what would it be?"

**Decision:**
- "Besides yourself, who else would weigh in on a decision like this?"
- "Have you tried any other solution for this? What happened?"
- "What's the cost of NOT solving this for another 6 months?"

## 5. DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT

**Duration:** 25–30 minutes | **Goal:** Uncover pain, quantify the gap, run the demo, close for a free trial.

---

### Opening (2 min)

> "Hey [NAME], thanks for making the time. Here's what I'd like to do in the next 25 minutes:
>
> First, I'll ask a few questions about how [COMPANY] handles leads and follow-up today — just so I can tailor everything to your world.
>
> Then I'll pull up our platform, plug in YOUR numbers, and show you exactly where revenue is slipping through the cracks — and what an AI team built specifically for [INDUSTRY] would recover.
>
> If it makes sense, we'll get your AI team live within 24 hours on a free 7-day trial. If it doesn't, no hard feelings. Fair?"

---

### Discovery — Current State (5 min)

> "Let's start with the basics."

Ask 3–4 questions from the **Universal** list and 2–3 from their **vertical-specific** list (Section 4). Listen actively. Take notes — you'll feed these numbers into the platform.

Key things to capture:
- Monthly lead volume
- Average job / case / treatment value
- Team size handling phones and follow-up
- Estimated close rate
- Response time
- Biggest single frustration

---

### Discovery — Pain Quantification (3 min)

Use what they told you to make the cost real:

> "So you said you get about [X] leads a month and your average job is $[Y]. If even 20 % of those are falling through the cracks from missed calls or slow follow-up, that's roughly $[MATH] per month walking out the door. Does that feel about right?"

> "And you mentioned [SPECIFIC PAIN THEY SHARED]. How long has that been going on?"

> "What have you tried to fix it?"

---

### Transition to Demo (30 sec)

> "Got it. Let me show you something. I'm going to pull up our platform, plug in your actual numbers, and show you exactly what this looks like for [COMPANY]. Give me one second to share my screen."

---

### Live Demo (15 min)

Follow the **Demo Flow** in Section 2, Steps 2–9.

**Key moments to nail:**

1. **Entering their numbers** — read the sliders out loud as you input. "46 assessment requests a month… average inspection value $625… that 41 % booking rate…" This makes it personal.

2. **Pain Reveal** — pause after each card. Ask: "Does this resonate?" Let them react.

3. **Day in Your Life** — this is the emotional turning point. Narrate both timelines. Make the before painful and the after effortless.

4. **ROI** — state the payback period clearly. "Your system pays for itself in [X] days."

---

### Objection Handling (as needed)

| They Say | You Say |
|---|---|
| **"How much does it cost?"** | "Before I give you a number — based on your numbers, you're leaving $[X]/month on the table. Our pricing is a fraction of that. More importantly, the 7-day trial is free. If it's not working, you walk away. The real question isn't what it costs — it's what it's costing you NOT to have this." |
| **"We tried AI before and it didn't work."** | "Most AI tools are generic — same for a dentist and a roofer. That's why they fail. Ours is built specifically for [INDUSTRY]. The agents know [INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC THING]. Can I show you the difference?" |
| **"I need to think about it."** | "Totally fair. What specifically would you be thinking through? … Instead of thinking in theory, let's just run the free trial. You'll have real data in 7 days to make a decision — not assumptions." |
| **"No time to implement."** | "You don't have to do anything — we handle the entire setup. Your AI team is live within 24 hours. This actually saves you time by taking follow-up off your plate." |
| **"We're doing fine."** | "Glad to hear it. If you're doing [X revenue] now with [Y %] of leads falling through the cracks, what happens if you capture those? Based on your numbers, there's $[Z]/month that's slipping away — not because you're doing anything wrong, but because humans can only do so much." |
| **"Send me some info."** | "Happy to. But a PDF won't show you the $[X]/month you're leaving on the table. The demo takes 15 minutes and uses your real numbers. If you don't see value, I'll stop wasting your time." |
| **"Need to talk to my partner."** | "Of course. Two options: I can do a 15-minute demo for both of you together, or you start the free trial and let the results speak for themselves. Which makes more sense?" |

### Trial Close (3 min)

**Step 1 — Summarise:**
> "So based on everything we just walked through, [COMPANY] is currently leaving about $[X]/month on the table from [TOP 2 PAIN POINTS]. With your AI team handling [SPECIFIC SOLUTIONS], you'd capture $[Y] in recovered revenue starting this month."

**Step 2 — Remove risk:**
> "Here's what I'm proposing: AI team live within 24 hours, completely free for 7 days. No payment, no commitment. If you're not seeing results, you walk away."

**Step 3 — Gentle urgency:**
> "Every day without this, that's roughly $[X ÷ 30] in leads slipping through. The sooner we start, the sooner it stops."

**Step 4 — Ask:**
> "Let's get started. Who should be the main point of contact for setup, and what's the best number to configure your AI team on?"
